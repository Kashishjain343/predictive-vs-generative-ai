# Predictive AI vs. Generative AI

A classroom mini-lesson prepared and presented by Kashish Kamble
at Brandeis International Business School.

## Overview
This presentation explains how predictive and generative AI differ
and how businesses can use them together.

## Topics covered
- Outputs, training data, and model behavior
- Regression, classification, and time-series forecasting
- Decision trees, random forests, and gradient boosting
- Transformers, attention, and diffusion models
- Business applications and combined workflows
